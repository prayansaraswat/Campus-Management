Engineered for University Support Workflows

Built according to student discovery insights and campus governance protocols.

Database-Driven Routing

Concerns are automatically routed according to an admin-editable database routing table, avoiding hardcoded assumptions.

Confidential Wellbeing Route

Wellbeing concerns are strictly restricted on the server to the student and authorized Wellbeing Staff, completely hidden from other staff and admin dashboards.

Escalation & Overdue SLA

Staff can escalate unresolvable concerns to higher authorities along configurable chains. Concerns unacknowledged past the limit are flagged overdue.

Cabin Slots & Appointments

Staff publish their cabin hours and availability slots. Students book slots directly without wasting time checking empty offices.

Non-Duplication of Facilities

Maintenance and housekeeping requests are guided straight to the existing university maintenance portal without duplicate ticket pipelines.

Transparent Notifications

Students and staff receive in-app notifications whenever status updates, comments, or appointment actions occur.
